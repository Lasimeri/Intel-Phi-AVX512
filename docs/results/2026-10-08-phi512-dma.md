# 2026-10-08: phi512 runs llama.phi's AVX-512F build, and the card's DMA engine carries its regions

The wrapper (`scripts/phi512.sh`: a program's AVX-512 regions executed
on a card, `docs/results/2026-09-22-seamless-card.md`) against llama.phi
built for AVX-512F alone (`build-avx512f`: `-mavx512f -mno-avx512vl
-mno-avx512bw -mno-avx512dq -mno-avx512cd`), on the GPU rack (EPYC 7742,
kernel 7.2.6-1-cachyos), **card 3** only, in two windows production lent
(cards 0 to 2 served meanwhile). Logs and scripts:
`/mnt/raid5/phi/bench/phi512-2026-10-08b/` on the rack (`before-llama.sh`,
`w1-*.sh`, `w2-*.sh`, `w3-restore.sh`, `e2e-*.sh`, `region-stats.c`, the
`xscan` scanner with the emulator's table, the `jknzd` card programs).
The model is Qwen3.8 Flash Next UD-Q4_K_XL (104 GB, mapped; production's
BF16 stays on the other cards), `llama-simple -n N "The capital of France
is"`, 4 threads (its default), greedy.

## The DMA change (`card/vpu/exec.md`, the 2026-10-08 section)

A phase's bytes used to cross in block-device requests to the host's
`phictl` (`vpu_pull`, `vpu_push`: a request per piece with its fixed
cost, on top of the mailbox round trip that has the host copy the
program's memory into the window). Now the worker's own DMA channel
(`cdma.S`, channel 7) pulls the bundle, the ranges-mode fetches and a
demand phase's code chunk into card memory (`exec_pull`: pieces of one
descriptor's length through the landing area, each waited for and
copied on in vectors), and a write-back slot of up to 32 KiB goes to the
host as posted vector stores through the window's mapping (the table's
used entries and the pages, a fence, then the mail). The demand fault
inside a run keeps the block device (a signal handler on a thread whose
vector registers are the program's). No protocol change; the host is as
it was.

Measured on card 3, `phi512.sh --card 3 --verbose` over the same program
(the build's `llama-simple -n 2`), the per-region lines summed by
`region-stats.c` (microseconds; fetch, run and write-back are the card's
phases, card is the card's total, wall the host's):

| worker | regions | fetch mean/median | run | write back | card total | wall | the stretch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| main's (bd39b6d, block device), first run after a start | 18609 | 177 / 156 | 28 / 23 | 110 / 107 | 1134 / 1104 | 1172 / 1139 | 21.8 s |
| this branch's (DMA), first run after a start | 18609 | 37 / 30 | 21 / 16 | 14 / 14 | 586 / 559 | 617 / 588 | 11.5 s |
| main's, second run, 651467 regions (8 minutes, cut) | 651467 | 58 / 0 | 22 / 21 | 30 / 0 | 556 / 503 | 573 / 511 | 373.7 s |
| this branch's, second run, the same first 651467 regions | 651467 | 11 / 0 | 19 / 16 | 3 / 0 | 308 / 270 | 329 / 277 | 214.9 s |

- The fetch falls 4 to 5 times and the write-back 8 to 10 times; the
  card's total per region falls 45 to 48 percent, the wall 43 to 47. Over
  the second runs the branch ran 1942 regions a second against 1357.
- What is left of a region (about 270 us at the median) is not the data:
  the descriptor bundle's read (one DMA now), the code and thunk pages'
  mapping and protection changes (each a TLB shootdown on 114 threads),
  the mailbox round trips (host polling), the region's end, and the
  descriptor's return. The worker's stage trace (`-v`) that splits those
  was not taken: the window did not allow the extra runs.
- The first run after a worker start stalls at request 18614 on both
  workers ("the card did not finish the phase within 60 s"); the second
  run goes through that request and on. Pre-existing, not the DMA; not
  diagnosed (the stall is at the same request each time, the worker
  survives it and serves the next run).

## End to end

The same prompt, three ways; the card path's two runs were capped at
300 and 600 s inside the window:

| path | tokens | result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the plain CPU build (`build-phi512-cpu`: the F-only build's ggml flags minus AVX-512, GGML_NATIVE off, 4 threads) | 8, 32 | **2.56, 2.38 tokens a second** generation (391, 420 ms a token), prompt 0.88, 0.78; the text: "The capital of France is Paris. The capital of Germany is Berlin. ..." |
| the AVX-512F build under `--emulate` (no card) | 2 | **0.05 tokens a second** (37.2 s for 2 tokens after a 9-minute load), the text "The capital of France is Paris.": the same two tokens as the CPU build's |
| the AVX-512F build on card 3 through the wrapper, this branch's worker | 0 | after 600 s still in `load_tensors`: llama.cpp repacks 42 GB of Q4_K weights at load (`CPU_REPACK`), region by region, 1.17 million regions in the 600 s at about 2000 a second; no token |

So the cards do not help this program through the instruction-level
path, and the DMA does not change that verdict: at 270 us a region and
millions of regions a token, the per-region floor is the cost, not the
link (the data's share of a region was 110 to 190 us and is 30 now). The
emulator, at about 2 us an instruction after a site is patched, is 18 s a
token; the CPU build 0.4 s. Weights crossing PCIe each call is not what
is measured here: a region's fetch is a few pages of activations and
stack, not the weights (the repack and the matmul loops read the weights
through the card's own demand paging or the declared ranges, a page at a
time); region-level caching of read-only pages across calls would cut
the fetch further but not the floor. For this model the way that helps
is the one already taken: `libggml_phi.so`, whole operators to the card
(`docs/results/2026-10-07-rack-generation.md`).
